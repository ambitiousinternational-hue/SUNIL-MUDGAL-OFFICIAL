ABOUT SUNIL MUDGAL

About Sunil Mudgal — Jaipur, Rajasthan

Sunil Mudgal is a Jaipur, Rajasthan–based public figure engaged in community interaction, social initiatives, public programmes and organisational activities.

His public journey has included participation in social organisations and engagement on issues concerning community participation, representation and bringing people together on a common platform.

In 2023, public reporting identified Sunil Mudgal of Brahmshakti Sangh among those associated with the Kesariya Mahapanchayat in Jaipur. The reporting included his comments concerning bringing people together on a common platform.

Public reporting has also identified Sunil Mudgal with the Environment Forest and Climate Change Promotion Council, including references to organisational leadership responsibilities.

His public activities span community engagement, organisational participation, public programmes and environmental initiatives. This website brings together selected photographs, public activities, media references and updates to provide a central profile of his public work and engagements.
