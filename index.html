Public Service & Stewardship

Shree Sunil Mudgal is a seasoned political leader and public servant with over 25 years of active engagement in student activism, organizational party building, and social advocacy across Rajasthan[cite: 1].


Beginning his public journey in student leadership at Rajasthan University in 1999[cite: 1], he has continuously worked to connect youth, farmers, and community leaders with constructive social development and governance initiatives[cite: 1].

Key Responsibilities

Political Journey

1999 – 2003
Student Leadership & Activism

Commenced public service as a student leader, actively organizing and leading student initiatives at Rajasthan University[cite: 1].

2003
Entry into Active Party Operations

Entered active party politics under the leadership of Smt. Vasundhara Raje Scindia, taking a key youth mobilization role in the state 'Parivartan Yatra'[cite: 1].

2007 – Present
Community Representation & Leadership

Appointed President of Akhil Bhartiya Brahmin Mahasabha, leading national social harmony and community welfare movements[cite: 1].

2019
Brahm Shakti Sangh & Maha Panchayat

Served as National Convener for the historic Brahmin Maha Panchayat held at Vidyadhar Nagar Stadium, Jaipur[cite: 1].

Kisan Outreach & Social Leadership

🌾 Kisan Morcha

Served as Rajasthan State Convener for Yuva Kisan Samiti[cite: 1]. Organized the massive 15,000+ farmer conference (Kisan Mahasammelan) in Deedwana (Nagaur) and contributed to key national campaigns in Gorakhpur and Haryana[cite: 1].

🤝 Brahm Shakti Sangh

Driving cultural preservation, social unity, and youth mentorship across India as National Convener of Brahm Shakti Sangh[cite: 1].

🏛️ Grassroots Outreach
