Public Leadership & Service

Shree Sunil Mudgal is a seasoned political leader and public servant with over two decades of active engagement in student activism, organizational party building, and social advocacy across Rajasthan.


Starting his political journey with student activism at Rajasthan University in 1999, he has dedicated his life to uniting community members, youth, and farmers to ensure social progress and equitable representation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Political Wing: Former National Executive Member, BJP Kisan Morcha
  • Youth Mobilization: Former State Convener, Yuva Kisan Samiti
  • Social Integration: National Convener, Brahm Shakti Sangh
  • Headquarters: Jaipur, Rajasthan

Political Journey

1999 – 2003
Student Leadership

Commenced public work leading student movements and student welfare initiatives at Rajasthan University.

2003
Active Entry & Parivartan Yatra

Participated in party mobilization during the state Parivartan Yatra under Smt. Vasundhara Raje Scindia.

2007 – Present
Community Service

Elected President of Akhil Bhartiya Brahmin Mahasabha, driving national community integration.

2019
Brahmin Maha Panchayat

Served as National Convener for the historic Maha Panchayat held at Vidyadhar Nagar Stadium, Jaipur.
