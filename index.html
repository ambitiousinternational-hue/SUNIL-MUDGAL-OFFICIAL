Public Service & Leadership

Committed to Public Service

Shri Sunil Mudgal has been associated with student leadership, organizational activities, community initiatives and social advocacy.

His public engagement has focused on connecting youth, farmers and community representatives with social development and public welfare initiatives.

Based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, his activities include grassroots outreach, community interaction, farmer-related initiatives and social harmony programs.
